# EspRadio: versions

What each version fixes and adds. The newest on top. Written in English: the notes are
shown as they are, in the console and on the server.

## 1.0.5 (2026-10-18)

- The names of its commands and settings, in English and Italian, travel with the console to a server; the phrases it has in common with its full version are kept once, with the same keys
- The settings tell their unit, and which ones are technical

## 1.0.4 (2026-10-14)

- The device tells how many favourite stations it keeps, to Home Assistant and to who reads it as data

## 1.0.3 (2026-10-11)

- No change in how it works: comments and texts of the code

## 1.0.2 (2026-10-10)

- From the remote console only the stations a browser can play there are listed. A page that comes by https cannot play a station that streams by http: those stations were shown and then refused. From the page of the device, at home, every station is listed as before

## 1.0.1 (2026-10-09)

- Two more tabs show, still, what EspWebMedia has, the full version of this radio: a television and a media player. A box on top tells whose they are and opens the shop
- The note under the radio says only what is set in the settings
- EspRadio and ExpNet no longer go together in one firmware: their pages are more than a device carries

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-08)

- First version: a radio of the web in the console. The sound comes out of the phone or PC that looks at the page
- Three looks, chosen with a key: a car stereo of matte black with lights of the colour you like, a stage amplifier, a neon sign
- One question to more than one directory at once (Radio Browser, SomaFM, laut.fm), and a reserve inside the page for when the large one does not answer
- One box to search: the words are looked for in the names and in the genres, and a country written there is the country. Genres and countries are chosen by writing a few letters. A country with little to give is followed by the rest of the world
- The most listened, the most voted, the trending ones, and the stations near the device
- Equalizer with ten bands and presets, the spectrum and two levels, on the stations that let the page read their sound; the others play without it, and the panel says so
- Favourites kept by the device, the same from every phone and PC, with categories of yours; the first six on the memory keys; export and import
- Before and next follow the list in sight; scan plays a few seconds of each station until you stop; sleep timer; the buttons of the lock screen and of the headphones; the last stations listened to; the title on air where the directory tells it
